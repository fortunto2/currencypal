Privacy Policy

CurrencyPal (CurrencyPals) for iOS · Last updated 30 July 2026

CurrencyPal does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

We cannot see who you are, what you convert, or how often you use the app. None of that information ever leaves your device, because the app never sends it anywhere.

What stays on your device

Everything the app remembers is stored locally on your iPhone and is removed when you delete the app:

  • The list of currencies you chose and their order
  • The most recently downloaded exchange rates, cached so the app works offline
  • Cached 30-day rate history for pairs whose chart you have opened

Amounts you type are held in memory only and are never written anywhere.

Network requests

The app makes exactly one kind of network request: fetching public exchange rates. Nothing about you is attached to these requests — no identifier, no account, no usage data.

  • frankfurter.dev — reference rates published by the European Central Bank
  • api.coingecko.com — cryptocurrency prices
  • open.er-api.com — rates for currencies the ECB does not publish

As with any internet request, these providers can see the IP address the request comes from and may log it under their own privacy policies. We receive nothing from them beyond the rates themselves, and we operate no server of our own.

Data we do not have

No name, no email, no phone number, no contacts, no location, no device identifiers, no advertising ID, no crash analytics, no behavioural data. Because we never collect it, there is nothing for us to share, sell, disclose, or lose.

Children

The app is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Rights such as access, correction, deletion, or portability (under the GDPR, CCPA, and similar laws) apply to data a company holds about you. We hold none. To erase everything the app has stored, delete the app from your device.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. A change that would involve collecting any data would also require a new app version, described in its release notes.
